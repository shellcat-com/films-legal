FAQ

Filmsera questions, answered

Plain answers about guests, pricing, the reveal, your photos and privacy.

Updated November 3, 2026

Guests

Do guests need to download the app?

No. Guests can join from the free Filmsera app on iPhone, or right in their phone's browser with no app and no account: they type a name and start shooting. Browser guests take photos only (no video), and the full film looks render live in the iPhone app. Guests never pay.

Does Filmsera work on Android?

Yes, for guests. Android guests join and shoot in their phone's browser, with no app or account. Browser guests take photos only (no video). Hosting a film, video clips and the 25 live film looks need the Filmsera app on iPhone (iOS 17 or later).

Do guests pay anything?

No. Guests never pay. They join with the free app and shoot into the host's film.

How do guests join a film?

The host shares the film's QR code, an invite link, or a short join code. Guests scan or tap it on their phone. With Filmsera installed on iPhone, the film opens in the app; otherwise guests can join right in the browser by typing a name.

How many guests can join one film?

In the current version of Filmsera there is no guest limit on a film, and hosting is free. Guests join from the iPhone app or from any phone's browser.

Pricing

How much does Filmsera cost?

Hosting and joining films is free. An optional Event Pass ($7.99 once, for one film) gives that film an HD recap without a watermark. Filmsera Pro, a subscription at $1.99 a week (with a 3-day free trial), $5.99 a month or $34.99 a year, unlocks all 25 cameras and every recap style. Prices are in USD.

Is it a subscription or a one-time purchase?

Both exist, and both are optional. Filmsera Pro is an auto-renewing subscription you can cancel in your Apple Account. The Event Pass is a one-time $7.99 purchase for a single film and never renews.

How do refunds work?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The reveal

Can I see the photos before the reveal?

No. Photos stay hidden while everyone shoots, like a real disposable camera. When the reveal time arrives, the whole film appears at once for everyone in it. The host can reveal early if the group can't wait.

Who can see the photos?

Only people in the film. Films are invite-only, and the photos appear for everyone in the film at the reveal, each tagged with who took it.

Photos

Can I save the photos?

Yes. After the reveal you can save photos to your iPhone and share the recap reel. Photos and clips in a shared film are kept on our servers for 30 days from the reveal. If the host buys an Event Pass for that film, they are kept for 1 year from the reveal. Film Pro does not change how long a film's photos are kept. Films created before Filmsera 4.2 are not removed automatically. Photos you save to your iPhone are not affected. Save anything you want to keep. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Is there video?

Yes. Hold the shutter to record a short clip through the current film look.

Privacy and account

Does Filmsera show ads, track me or train AI on my photos?

No. Filmsera shows no ads, uses no third-party tracking, and does not use your photos to train AI models. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored and why.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from inside the app. The Privacy Policy explains what is removed and when.

What languages is Filmsera in?

Filmsera is available in 21 languages, and you can switch language inside the app.
