Holiday parties

A shared camera for the holiday party

Friendsgiving, the office party, Christmas and New Year's Eve: one roll for everyone, revealed together.

Updated November 3, 2026

Filmsera gives a holiday party one shared camera. Guests scan a QR code and shoot on their own phones, in the app or the browser, and every photo stays hidden until the reveal time you pick, like midnight on New Year's Eve. Then the whole night appears at once. Hosting a film for up to 10 guests is free, and guests never pay.

After the reveal, Filmsera makes a vertical recap reel with music, ready to share with the people who couldn't make it.

Guests on any phone

The host creates the film in the Filmsera app on iPhone. Guests can join from any phone. With the free Filmsera app on iPhone they get the full experience: 25 film looks live in the viewfinder, video clips and recaps. Everyone else, Android included, scans the same QR code and joins in the phone's browser, with no app and no account: they type a name and shoot.

Browser guests take photos only (no video), with a light film grade rather than the live looks. Their photos land in the same roll and reveal with everyone else's. Our side-by-side comparison shows how this compares with other event cameras.

Holiday party questions

What's a good reveal time for a holiday party?

Pick a moment the group will share: midnight on New Year's Eve, the morning after the office party, or Boxing Day morning for a Christmas get-together. You set it when you create the film.

Can we use Filmsera instead of renting a photo booth?

For many parties, yes. Photo Booth mode in the Filmsera iPhone app shoots four-shot strips, and the strips go into the same film as everyone else's photos.

How many guests can join one film?

A free film holds up to 10 guests, or 20 if the host has Filmsera Pro. For more, the host buys a one-time pass for that film: Party ($7.99, up to 30 guests), Celebration ($24.99, up to 100) or Wedding ($49.99, up to 250). The host can upgrade a film to a bigger pass later. Guests join from the iPhone app or from any phone's browser.

Do guests need to download the app?

No. Guests can join from the free Filmsera app on iPhone, or right in their phone's browser with no app and no account: they type a name and start shooting. Browser guests take photos only (no video), and the full film looks render live in the iPhone app. Guests never pay.

Does Filmsera work on Android?

Yes, for guests. Android guests join and shoot in their phone's browser, with no app or account. Browser guests take photos only (no video). Hosting a film and the 25 live film looks need the Filmsera app on iPhone (iOS 17 or later); video clips also need a Celebration or Wedding film.
