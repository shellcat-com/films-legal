How Filmsera works

From creating a film to the morning-after reveal, step by step.

Updated November 2, 2026

Filmsera is a shared disposable camera app for iPhone. A host creates a film for an event and guests join by QR code, link or short code. Everyone shoots from their own phone, and the photos stay hidden until the reveal time the host picks. Then the whole roll appears at once, tagged with who took each shot.

Six steps, start to reveal

1. Create a film

The host names the film, picks one of 25 film looks for the whole roll, sets the roll size and chooses when it reveals.

2. Invite your guests

Share the film's QR code, an invite link or a short join code. Print the QR on a sign or send the link to the group chat.

3. Guests join on their iPhones

Scanning the code opens the film in the free Filmsera app, or the App Store if it isn't installed. Guests need an iPhone and the app; they never pay.

4. Everyone shoots

Photos go straight into the shared roll and stay hidden, like a real disposable. Hold the shutter for a short clip, or switch to Photo Booth for four-shot strips.

5. The reveal

A countdown runs until the reveal time, including a Live Activity on the lock screen. Then the whole roll develops at once, each photo tagged with who took it.

6. Recap and save

Filmsera cuts the roll into a vertical recap reel with music for Stories and Reels. Save the photos and the reel to your iPhone.

What the host controls

  • The look: one of 25 film looks, shared by the whole roll.
  • The reveal time: the end of the night, the morning after, or days later. The host can also reveal early.
  • The roll size: how many shots each guest gets, so every photo counts.
  • Who's in: films are invite-only; only people with the code or link can join.

What guests need

An iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and the free Filmsera app. That's all: no account fees, no payment. Android phones and web browsers can't join a Filmsera film, so check your guest list first.

Privacy

Films are invite-only. Filmsera shows no ads, uses no third-party tracking, and does not use your photos to train AI models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Quick answers

Do guests need to download the app?

Yes. Every Filmsera guest needs an iPhone and the free Filmsera app. Scanning the QR code opens the app, or the App Store if it isn't installed yet. Guests don't need to pay or buy anything to join and shoot.

Does Filmsera work on Android?

No. Filmsera is an iPhone app (iOS 17 or later). Android phones and web browsers can't join a Filmsera film. If many of your guests use Android, an app with browser-based guest join will suit your event better; our comparison page lists some.

How do guests join a film?

The host shares the film's QR code, an invite link, or a short join code. Guests scan or tap it on their iPhone, and the film opens in Filmsera, ready to shoot.

Can I see the photos before the reveal?

No. Photos stay hidden while everyone shoots, like a real disposable camera. When the reveal time arrives, the whole film appears at once for everyone in it. The host can reveal early if the group can't wait.
