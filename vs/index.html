Event cameras at a glance

All four let guests join without installing a full app. Filmsera guests join in any phone's browser, Android included, with no account; browser guests shoot photos only, and the iPhone app adds the live film looks and video. Filmsera hosts create films in the iPhone app. Filmsera's strengths are 25 live film looks, a recap reel, and free hosting with no guest limit in the current version.
