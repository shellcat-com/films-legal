Filmsera vs Lense

Two shared event cameras, compared on facts. Prices and features checked November 2, 2026.

Updated November 2, 2026

The short version

Lense is the better fit for mixed iPhone and Android crowds: guests take photos without downloading an app, and Lense says it works on both platforms. It also offers a live photo display for a TV or projector and camera-roll uploads. Filmsera is the better fit for iPhone groups who want 25 live film looks, a recap reel, and free hosting with no guest limit in the current version.

Price by guest count

Lense's App Store list prices a 100-guest event at $34.99, or $44.99 with unlimited shots. In the current version of Filmsera, hosting a film for any number of guests is free; the optional $7.99 Event Pass adds an HD recap without a watermark.

Choose Lense if…

  • Some guests use Android, or won't install an app.
  • You want photos on a big screen during the event.
  • Guests should be able to add photos from their camera roll.

Choose Filmsera if…

  • Your guests are on iPhone and happy to install a free app.
  • You want the roll shot through a film look, live in the viewfinder.
  • You want a recap reel for Stories and Reels.

Questions

Does Lense work for Android guests?

Lense's website says it “works on both iPhone and Android devices,” and its App Store listing says guests don't need to download an app. Filmsera guests need an iPhone and the free Filmsera app.

How long does Lense keep photos?

Lense's website says photos and videos remain available for 12 months from the event start date.
