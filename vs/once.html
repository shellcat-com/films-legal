Filmsera vs Once

Two shared event cameras, compared on facts. Prices and features checked November 2, 2026.

Updated November 2, 2026

The short version

Once is the better fit when guests shouldn't have to install the app: its App Clip lets guests join with or without it. Filmsera is the better fit for iPhone groups who want 25 live film looks, a recap reel, and free hosting with no guest limit in the current version.

How guests join

Once: guests join through an App Clip, a small part of the app that runs without a full install, or with the full app.

Filmsera: guests scan the QR code or tap the invite link, and it opens the free Filmsera app (or the App Store). Every guest needs an iPhone.

Choose Once if…

  • Guests shouldn't have to install a full app.
  • You want to pay per event by participant count.

Choose Filmsera if…

  • Your guests are on iPhone and happy to install a free app.
  • You want a distinct film look, live in the viewfinder.
  • You want a recap reel for Stories and Reels.

Is Once free?

Once's App Store listing doesn't show a free tier; its smallest listed purchase is a 10-participant film for $1.99.
