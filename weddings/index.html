Weddings

A disposable camera for every wedding guest

One shared roll for the whole wedding. Guests shoot on their own phones, and nobody sees a photo until the reveal.

Updated November 3, 2026

Filmsera turns every guest's phone into a shared disposable camera for your wedding. Guests scan one QR code and shoot into the same roll, and the photos stay hidden until the reveal time you pick, often the morning after. Then the whole day appears at once, tagged with who took each shot. Hosting a film is free, with no guest limit, and guests never pay.

How it works at a wedding

  1. Create the film. Name it for the day, pick one film look for the whole roll, and set the reveal time.
  2. Print the QR code. Put it on the welcome sign, table cards or the bar, anywhere guests actually look.
  3. Guests shoot. Every photo goes into the same roll, unseen. Nobody can scroll back through the night.
  4. Reveal the morning after. The whole wedding appears at once, for everyone, each shot tagged with who took it.

What you get

  • Every angle in one place: the dance floor, the speeches, the table you never got to visit.
  • Who took what: every photo is tagged with the guest who shot it.
  • A recap reel: a vertical video with music, sized for Stories and Reels.
  • A reveal countdown: a Live Activity on the lock screen counts down to the reveal.
  • One look for the day: choose from 25 film looks; the whole roll shares it, so the photos feel like one set.

Guests on any phone

The host creates the film in the Filmsera app on iPhone. Guests can join from any phone. With the free Filmsera app on iPhone they get the full experience: 25 film looks live in the viewfinder, video clips and recaps. Everyone else, Android included, scans the same QR code and joins in the phone's browser, with no app and no account: they type a name and shoot.

Browser guests take photos only (no video), with a light film grade rather than the live looks. Their photos land in the same roll and reveal with everyone else's. Our side-by-side comparison shows how this compares with other event cameras.

Wedding questions

How do wedding guests join?

Put the film's QR code on the welcome sign and table cards. Guests scan it with their phone camera and join in the browser with no app or account, or in the free Filmsera app on iPhone for the live film looks and video. The host can also share an invite link or a short code.

When should the wedding photos reveal?

Most couples pick the morning after, so everyone wakes up to the whole night at once. You set the reveal time when you create the film, and the host can reveal early if the group can't wait.

How many guests can join one film?

In the current version of Filmsera there is no guest limit on a film, and hosting is free. Guests join from the iPhone app or from any phone's browser.

Does Filmsera work on Android?

Yes, for guests. Android guests join and shoot in their phone's browser, with no app or account. Browser guests take photos only (no video). Hosting a film, video clips and the 25 live film looks need the Filmsera app on iPhone (iOS 17 or later).

Can I save the photos?

Yes. After the reveal you can save photos to your iPhone and share the recap reel. Photos and clips in a shared film are kept on our servers for 30 days from the reveal. If the host buys an Event Pass for that film, they are kept for 1 year from the reveal. Film Pro does not change how long a film's photos are kept. Films created before Filmsera 4.2 are not removed automatically. Photos you save to your iPhone are not affected. Save anything you want to keep. The Privacy Policy has the details.
